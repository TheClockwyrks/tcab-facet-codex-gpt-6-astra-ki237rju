# Facet

A quiet bench. Seven kinds of cut stone. One beautiful chain reaction. Take hold of a gem, carry it onto its neighbor, and release to make a line of three or more. The stones shatter, new ones fall, and the board reveals its next possibility.

Every clear leaves pressure behind. Nearby stones crack, fracture, and become flawed. Clear beside a flawed gem and it breaks too, scoring double and passing the shatter into the next weak spot. Learn to read the damage, then choose the move that lets it all go.

Longer lines leave something precious. Four stones create a brilliant that breaks the ring around it. Crossing lines make a star that sweeps its row and column. Five or more produce a kindless prism: trade it with a stone to take every gem of that kind.

Let the falling stones carry your chain higher, with each step worth more than the last. Meet the level’s target, admire your best move, and return to a fresh board. There is no timer to race. Keep finding possibilities until the stones have no moves left.
